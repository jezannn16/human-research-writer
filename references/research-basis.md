## Numbers that cannot be reinterpreted

The PDF provided describes a corpus of 61,608 fictional stories across 10,272 assignments, with human texts and generations of five LLMs (p. 1). The narrative model macro-F1 for a binary task is 93.2% (p. 1, 6). This is a metric on the experimental corpus, and not the share of “discovered coursework” or the probability of authorship of an individual text.

Explicit commentary by the narrator on the topic is indicated for 77% of machine and 52% of human stories (p. 7). The difference is 25 percentage points; it cannot be called a 25% increase without specifying a relative basis.

After specific stylistic edits, 278 Gemini macro-F1 stories change from 95.5 to 93.9% (p. 8). This is not a result for all models, not a test of all types of editing, and not an experiment on Russian-language coursework.
